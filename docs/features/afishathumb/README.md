## Visual contract

### Set: the column

A single 3D scene in Blender, no 2D composite. The column is modelled from scratch following the geometry spec at the bottom of [afishathumb-requirements.md](../../backlog/features/afishathumb/afishathumb-requirements.md) (sections 1–4). The unit `D` = diameter of the main cylindrical body; the whole column is `~4.5 D` tall.

- **Base (цоколь):** two stepped cast-iron tiers (plinth `Ø ~1.1 D × 0.08 D` + upper tier `Ø ~1.05 D × 0.1 D` with `~45°` bevel narrowing to `1.0 D`). Material: matte dark-green cast iron `#2A3B2C`, dirt/weathering near the ground.
- **Body:** perfectly straight cylinder `Ø = 1.0 D × ~2.5 D`. Material under the posters is a smooth light-grey plaster/concrete `#B0B5B0`. Visible plaster only at base/cornice gaps between posters.
- **Decorative belt (cornice):** rounded molding ring `Ø ~1.03 D × 0.04 D`, then smooth frieze `1.0 D × ~0.4 D` with a recessed information plaque on the front face (`~0.4 D × 0.25 D`, light `#D9DDD8` background, dark serif type), then a supporting bell-curve cornice flaring to `Ø ~1.2 D` at the top (`~0.25 D` tall, 2–3 thin horizontal grooves). All cornice metalwork shares the `#2A3B2C` cast-iron material.
- **Dome + spire:** ogee dome `~0.7 D` tall with a fish-scale tile pattern (smaller scales near the top, larger near the base, half-step staggered courses); copper-verdigris `#6C8E75` material, matte, with AO darkening in the seams and lighter wear on highlights. On top: a small base ring (`Ø ~0.15 D`), a flattened bulb, and an upward-pointing spike — total spire height `~0.35 D`.

Reference image: [docs/backlog/features/afishathumb/afishathumb-ref.png](../../backlog/features/afishathumb/afishathumb-ref.png). Reconstruct the silhouette faithfully — proportions and material story are not negotiable.

### Background

The column stands on the Kaliningrad embankment background: [docs/backlog/features/afishathumb/fon.png](../../backlog/features/afishathumb/fon.png). The background is a high-resolution still plate (no animation). It is used either as an environment HDRI-like flat plane, a camera-aligned backplate, or an emissive backdrop — implementation choice TBD, but it must not interact with column lighting (no harsh wash, no colour bleed strong enough to make posters unreadable). See lighting rule below.

### Posters and stickers (the "глей" grammar)

Posters and information stickers are glued onto the cylinder body procedurally. Layout rules:

- The cylinder is **almost fully covered** with posters; small bare patches showing the painted plaster are allowed and desirable so the iron base feel reads.
- Posters are **varied in size**, glued not on a strict grid, with mild rotation jitter. Some overlap or touch their neighbours; some have **peeling corners** (paper curl, 1–3 corners max per poster).
- A subset of posters carries **light crumpling** (paper texture wrinkles). Reuse the existing crumple deformation from `CrumpleVideo` but only its **near-fully-unfolded** variant — afishathumb posters are flat-glued, not folded. Most posters are not crumpled at all.
- A poster may be **image-only** (no text on the print itself). In that case the event title moves to a **neighbour sticker** (typographic label glued next to the poster — clearly visually grouped with it, not floating between two unrelated posters).
- **Information stickers** sit next to their poster and carry, with strong typography, whatever the poster does not already show: title (if image-only), date + time, location name + address + city, price / "бесплатно" flag. Visual grouping (adjacency, shared rotation, sometimes a sticker partly overlapping its own poster) must make the bond unambiguous.
- **Multi-image events:** up to 4 images per event, glued together as a cluster on the cylinder. One is the **primary** (camera lands on it); secondaries can be passed over in the camera flight. Each release must lay out clusters slightly differently — no fixed template.
- **`search_digest` fallback:** when an event's only visual is a generic picture (no poster info), an adjacent sticker may carry the event's `search_digest` (a 16–20-word machine-extracted description already stored on the event). Use sparingly.
- **Excursions** from the guide-excursions digest may be glued onto the column too, with priority for excursions that already have a poster. Excursions without a poster get the same `image + info-sticker` cluster treatment.
- Tidy on average. Natural imperfection (slight tilt, peeled corner, a wrinkle) — yes; chaos — no.

### Promo slots

- Promo events get a special **"обратите внимание"** sticker glued near (and sometimes partly across the edge of) the promo poster. The sticker has variable placement — not a fixed badge slot.
- Per release: at most **1–2 promo slots visible** as anchors; additional promo posters may sit on the cylinder but the camera only passes over them without dwelling.
- A promo slot must **not be the first** anchor of the release. Best position is the second.
- **Never two promo anchors back-to-back.**

### Lighting

- One natural-key + soft fill lighting setup matching the daytime mood of [fon.png](../../backlog/features/afishathumb/fon.png).
- **No overexposed surfaces.** If a poster's text would become unreadable because the highlight clips, the lighting is wrong — fix the lighting, do not silently lose text. This is a hard quality gate during stills review (see Debug pipeline below).

### Camera

- The camera **never fully stops**. During "reading" moments it slows down to a near-still drift but keeps moving.
- Movement is eased everywhere: ease-in, ease-out, ease-in-out, and occasional more aggressive ease curves for fast traversals between distant slots. No linear motion plateaus.
- Mix of motion scales: subtle micro-pushes during info reading; medium dolly+truck moves between adjacent posters; occasional faster flights around the cylinder for non-adjacent slot order.
- Multi-image event flight: camera lands on the primary image first, then a closer push to the supporting info sticker — mirroring how a person naturally scans the cluster. Secondaries may be passed over en route.
- The camera **orbits the cylinder** during the run. Up to 6 main slots are read on-screen per release (the cylinder may carry more posters than this — extras are dressing).
- The full path and per-slot timing must be **pre-computed** before render so cadence is auditable.
- Start of the run: an **establishing wide** matching the reference framing (the whole column in shot, on the embankment), then a glide in + sideways shift onto the first anchor slot.

### Outro

The cherryflash three-strip slide-in outro is **replaced** by a two-poster ending **glued on the same cylinder**:

- Sticker 1: **«Полюбить Калининград Анонсы»** (the brand line, matching the last-frame style from CrumpleVideo's final).
- Sticker 2: **«Мост в Кёнигсберг»**.
- Glued side by side or with mild overlap so that **for part of the outro both are visible in the same frame**.

The outro is part of the cylinder render; it does not break out into a separate 2D scene.

## Selection contract

Inherits CherryFlash's `popular_review` selection, extended.

- Source: `/popular_posts`-style popularity pool with weekly anti-repeat, scoped by `profile_key=afishathumb` (so AfishaThumb's repeats are independent of CherryFlash's).
- **Up to 6 main events** read on-camera. The cylinder may dress in additional poster-events that are visible-but-not-read.
- **Future-start-only** rule from CherryFlash applies: events whose start date is before today are excluded.
- **Renderability gate** from CherryFlash applies: catbox-only events are dropped unless source-specific rehydrate (Telegram public page poster, VK `wall.getById`) finds a non-catbox poster.
- **Multi-image events:** keep up to **4** poster images per event for cluster layout.
- **Image-only posters** are allowed (no `ocr_text` requirement) — title comes from `event.title`, supporting info comes from event fields, optional `search_digest` sticker fills gaps.
- **Excursions from the guide-excursions digest** are eligible. Priority: those with a poster. Without a poster, image + stickers + `search_digest` treatment.
- **Promo injection:** reuse CherryFlash promo injection (up to two eligible promo events per run), but apply afishathumb-specific placement rules (not first, never two in a row, 1–2 anchored).
- **Sold-out events** are excluded.

### Per-slot data the render needs

Per main slot:

- `event_id`, `title`, `poster_urls[]` (1..4 primary + secondaries), `ocr_text` (may be empty for image-only),
- `date_iso`, `time`, `city`, `location_name`, `location_address`,
- `price_text` and/or `is_free` flag,
- `search_digest` (16–20 words),
- `promo` flag + provenance (`video_general` etc.),
- `is_excursion` flag.

Promo placement order is decided **after** raw selection, by a small layout planner that obeys the position constraints above.

## Timing contract

- **Intro** duration `≤ CherryFlash intro`.
- Per-slot timing taken from CherryFlash slot timing as the baseline. Slots with high information density (long titles, multi-image clusters, info-rich stickers) may run **slightly longer**, but the total release length should stay in CherryFlash's envelope.
- **Outro** duration similar to CherryFlash outro, but the two stickers must co-exist visibly for a non-trivial fraction of that time.

## Debug and rollout pipeline

Per requirements, this product is rolled out in four explicit stages. Each stage gates the next.

### Stage 1 — Honest selection probe

- Run today's selection layer with the AfishaThumb extensions on the real production data.
- Print: ordered list of up to 6 main slots + extras-on-cylinder + promo + excursions, with for each slot: `event_id`, title, poster URLs, `ocr_text` presence, `search_digest`, date/time/location/city, price/free, promo and excursion flags.
- No render. The output is the input to Stage 2.

### Stage 2 — Per-slot stills

- Local Blender. For each slot from Stage 1, render **one still** showing how that slot would look on the cylinder: the poster (with peel/wrinkle if any) and its info stickers in their final positions, with the production lighting.
- Self-evaluate each still against the requirements before showing it. Hard rejection criteria:
  - any text on the poster or its stickers is unreadable due to overexposure / shadow / scale,
  - the info stickers visually attach to the **wrong** poster (ambiguity test: would a stranger think this sticker belongs to the neighbour?),
  - the cluster looks too neat (no peel/wrinkle anywhere) or too chaotic (most posters peeling),
  - the supporting info on the stickers is incomplete (missing date/time or missing location for an event that has those fields populated).
- Only present passers. One-line note per still on what it demonstrates.

### Stage 3 — Camera pass (fast renders)

- Once stills are accepted, generate **fast / draft** renders of the full camera flight per release. Quality is intentionally low; the goal is to validate motion: continuity, eases, focus order (image → info), traversal logic between slots, intro/outro framing.
- Iterate motion until cadence reads "premium" rather than mechanical.

### Stage 4 — High-quality render on Kaggle

- Move the production-quality render to Kaggle (GPU where available, CPU fallback) using the cherryflash-style dataset/kernel handoff.
- Wire test publish to `@keniggpt`, then production stories to `@mostvkenig` only after operator confirmation.

## Per-slot scene design (algorithm, awaiting operator review)

This section is the **design document for one event's slot** — the recipe the implementation will follow when rendering a single event on the column. Operator review wanted on the algorithm BEFORE the next round of code. A separate section below covers the route between slots (multi-event flight).

### Goal of one slot

Within `≈ 5.7 s` (CherryFlash slot envelope), deliver to the viewer six information pieces about ONE event with enough legibility for them to decide whether to attend:

1. **image** — visual hook (the poster)
2. **title** — event name
3. **essence** — what happens
4. **when** — date + time
5. **where** — venue + city
6. **cost** — free or price

Camera language: minimum movements, eased throughout, "expensive-looking", continuous slide rather than discrete stops. No more than 1 attention dwell that involves a "focus push" (closer than main framing).

### Inputs (per event)

| Source | Field | Use |
|---|---|---|
| `event` DB row | `title`, `date`, `time`, `location_name`, `location_address`, `city`, `is_free`, `ticket_price_min/max`, `search_digest` | canonical text content |
| `event.photo_urls[0]` (after renderability rehydrate) | poster image bytes | the glued artwork |
| `EventPoster.ocr_text` (when present) | text on poster | fallback for "is info already on poster" |
| **LLM region detector (Gemini-lite)** | per-region normalised bbox: `title`, `date`, `time`, `location`, `price`, each `[x0,y0,x1,y1]` or `null` | precise positions of info ALREADY on the poster — drives sticker decisions AND camera focus targets (see §"LLM gateway" below) |
| cv2 text-density mask | global density + integral image | fallback when LLM offline + sticker-placement validator |

### LLM region detector contract (Gemini-lite)

- **Model**: `gemini-3.1-flash-lite` (project canonical Lite, supports vision).
- **Prompt**: ask for a single JSON object with keys `title`, `date`, `time`, `location`, `price` — each value either `null` (not visibly present on the poster) or `[x0, y0, x1, y1]` in normalised `0..1` coordinates with origin top-left.
- **Retries**: up to 3 with exponential backoff per existing `google_ai/client.py` conventions.
- **Caching**: result persisted to `slot_<event_id>/poster_regions.json` so re-runs of the slot don't re-burn the quota.
- **Fallback**: when the API is offline / rate-limited / returns invalid JSON, fall back to the cv2 text-density heuristic with the same return shape but only `title` and a generic `dense_text` bbox.
- **`gpt-4o`** stays reserved for sparing use (e.g. validating a borderline placement decision); not on the hot per-slot path.

### Decision tree per information piece

For each of the six pieces, decide whether it comes from **POSTER** or from a **STICKER**, then where the camera reads it:

| Piece | If LLM returns a non-null bbox on the poster | Otherwise |
|---|---|---|
| image | always POSTER (the whole poster is the image) | — |
| title | POSTER — camera dwell over `title` bbox | STICKER beside poster |
| essence | POSTER + slow drift if poster has enough text in body (cv2 density > 0.30); otherwise STICKER beside poster with `search_digest` | STICKER with `search_digest` |
| when | POSTER — focus push over `date`+`time` union bbox | STICKER beside poster |
| where | almost always STICKER beside poster (rare poster carries full venue) | STICKER beside poster |
| cost | STICKER (yellow «БЕСПЛАТНО» or price card) — small, in a free corner of the poster or adjacent bare cylinder | STICKER |

Constraint: **never create a sticker for an info piece that LLM confirms is already legibly on the poster**. The camera dwells on the on-poster region instead.

### Sticker placement algorithm

Goal: TIGHT cluster around the poster, no covering of any LLM-detected info region, no paper-through-paper intersections, no sticker more than the event's chosen angular budget away from the poster edge. The budget is **seeded-random per event in `[3°, 15°]`** so each event gets a different cluster character (loose vs tight) without templating.

```
1. Compute poster anchor on cylinder:
     anchor_angle = 0°  (always +X-facing slot)
     anchor_z     = chosen vertical centre (1.55..1.80 depending on event_id seed)
     poster_w     = min(0.66, 0.50 * aspect)        (arc cap ~75°)
     poster_h     = poster_w / aspect               (preserve aspect)

2. Build OCCUPIED mask = LLM regions ∪ cv2 high-density cells.
   Convert to normalised poster space (0..1).

3. For each required sticker (in priority order: location, cost, digest/essence,
   then date if not on poster, title if not on poster):

   a. Candidate slots (8 sectors around the poster):
        TL, T, TR  — above the poster on bare cylinder
        L, R       — beside the poster on bare cylinder
        BL, B, BR  — below the poster on bare cylinder
        + on-poster sectors that DO NOT overlap OCCUPIED

   b. Score each candidate:
        - intrusion_score   = overlap with OCCUPIED  (× big weight)
        - collision_score   = overlap with previously placed stickers  (× big weight)
        - cluster_score     = angular distance from poster edge  (penalise > 22°)
        - side_balance      = prefer side opposite previous sticker
        - jitter            = seeded RNG(event_id) so events differ

   c. Pick lowest-score candidate. Record its bbox.

4. Assign tilt: small per-sticker rotation around its surface normal, seeded
   by event_id. PIL-side rotation is forbidden (creates black bbox corners) —
   tilt happens in the 3D mesh only.

5. Validate cluster:
     - all stickers within ±22° angular from poster edge
     - no two stickers overlap
     - no sticker overlaps any LLM region
     - poster peel respects readability (at most one strong + one mild corner)
```

### Camera path for ONE slot (≈ 5.7 s)

One continuous, eased motion — NOT a sequence of jumps. The camera follows a **single smooth arc** that visits **2 or 3 attention anchors** along its path (count chosen per event by seeded RNG `event_id`).

**Camera grammar (round-7 refinement):** the arc must NOT be a flat dolly that stays perpendicular to the cylinder axis. To read "expensive" / cinematic, the per-beat poses must vary on multiple axes:

- **dolly + truck**: in/out distance from the cylinder axis combined with a lateral slide along the orbital direction.
- **crane / boom**: vertical rise or fall during a beat — the camera can climb above the cluster on entry and settle down toward the info-sticker stack, or vice versa.
- **tilt**: pitch up/down at the moment of arrival so the framing reads slightly "above-looking-down" or "below-looking-up", not always head-on.
- **angled approach**: when pushing in toward an attention anchor, approach at `15–30°` off the radial axis so the poster reveals depth (we see the cylinder curve at the edge of the frame).
- **subtle roll** (only when justified): a tiny dutch-tilt during a fast traversal between distant slots, never inside a dwell.

All of these are eased (no constant-velocity moves) and are *combined* during a single beat — e.g. "push in + boom down + tilt down by 4°" while reading the info sticker stack feels like a real cinematographer's hand, not a turntable.

### LLM-driven camera path planning (Gemini lite, design)

Once a slot's papers and LLM-detected info regions are known, a Gemini-lite call can produce the per-event camera keypoints + a reading order that respects the storytelling priorities. The deterministic path planner is the fallback; LLM is the upgrade for richer event-specific motion.

**Inputs to the LLM call:**
- The full layout manifest snippet: list of papers (poster + extras + each sticker) with their `(angle, z, w, h)` on the cylinder and their semantic role (`image / title / when / where / cost / digest`).
- The LLM-detected poster info regions in normalised poster space (`title / date / time / location / price` bboxes).
- The event metadata in plain text (title, date, where, free/price, search_digest).
- The slot budget: `5.5–6.0 s`, `2 or 3` attention anchors per `event_id` seed, `tightness_deg` for the cluster.

**Output JSON contract (strict):**
```json
{
  "beats": [
    {
      "role": "entry|image|title|when|where|cost|essence|exit",
      "target_anchor": "paper-name OR llm_region:title|date|time|location|price",
      "target_offset_norm": [dx, dy],
      "dwell_s": 0.5,
      "lens_mm": 50,
      "camera_radius": 2.5,
      "camera_z_offset": 0.2,
      "camera_angle_offset_deg": 12,
      "tilt_deg": -4,
      "crane_motion": "rise|fall|hold",
      "approach_curve": "ease_in_out|ease_out|ease_in_fast"
    }
  ],
  "narrative": "one short sentence explaining the read order chosen"
}
```

`target_anchor` may be either a paper name from the manifest OR a token of the form `llm_region:<key>` — meaning "aim at the on-poster bbox of `<key>`", which is how we let the camera focus precisely on, say, where the date is printed on the poster rather than at the poster's geometric centre.

**Retries**: same `gemini-3.1-flash-lite` retry/cache pattern as `poster_llm.py`. Validates output JSON against the schema; falls back to the deterministic planner on parse failure or coverage-test failure.

**Coverage validation**: regardless of LLM authorship, the rendered keypoints must satisfy the per-slot tests in the "Validation tests" section above. The deterministic planner stays in the loop as the validator and the fallback.

**Status**: **design recorded, implementation deferred** until the per-slot visual layer is stable (round 7 stickering is now ready). Operator decision point: greenlight LLM camera planning OR keep the deterministic planner with crane-shot variation baked into the parametric beat positions.

```
t = 0.0s  CAMERA ENTRY
            position: medium-wide, slightly off-axis from anchor_angle,
                      framed so cluster fills ~70% of frame
            speed:    moderate, decelerating (ease-out from inter-slot transit)

t ≈ 0.6s  ATTENTION ANCHOR 1 — IMAGE
            focus point: poster image centre (or "image" focal point — the
                         most visually striking sub-region of the poster)
            motion:      gentle dolly-in + tiny lateral drift toward TITLE
            duration:    ~1.6 s of near-still slow drift (camera never stops)

t ≈ 2.2s  CONTINUOUS DRIFT TO ATTENTION ANCHOR 2 — KEY INFO
            "key info" = the info piece that DEFINES the event.
            Priority order to pick the key info:
              promo+ATTENTION sticker if present (focus push)
              else date+time bbox (on poster or sticker)
              else free/price sticker
              else location sticker
            motion:   continuous slide of the camera along the cluster,
                      slight push-in if focus is small text;
                      eased acceleration mid-motion, eased deceleration on arrival
            duration: ~0.9 s drift + ~1.4 s near-still focus

t ≈ 4.5s  EXIT GLIDE TOWARD NEXT SLOT
            motion:   pull radius slightly outward (≤ +25%) while sliding
                      angularly toward the next slot's anchor angle;
                      NO full pull-out to wide establishing — that belongs
                      only to the intro
            duration: ~1.2 s

t = 5.7s  END OF SLOT — handoff to next slot
```

Story coverage rule: ALL info pieces NOT covered by the two anchors must be **readable in passing**. Stickers that fall in the camera's path during the drift get drive-by attention; they're still in frame, the viewer's eye registers them. If any required info piece (per the matrix above) would not be readable at any point in this arc, **the placement step picks a different sticker location** — placement and camera are co-designed.

### Validation tests per slot (deterministic, run before render)

Run automatically per slot:

1. **info coverage**: each of `image / title / essence / when / where / cost` has at least one beat where the corresponding region (on poster or sticker) subtends `≥ 12%` of the frame's longer dimension at the beat's framing.
2. **arc smoothness**: camera angle / radius / z monotonic on each segment between anchors (no zig-zag).
3. **no occlusion**: at each beat, the focal target is in the cylinder hemisphere visible to the camera (angular delta from camera-axis-to-anchor ≤ 90°).
4. **placement legality**: no sticker overlaps an LLM info region of the poster; no sticker–sticker overlap.
5. **dwell budget**: `Σ dwell_s ≤ 5.7 s` (cherryflash baseline; ±0.6 s for high-information events).
6. **peel readability**: poster's peeled corners do not cover the LLM `title` or `date` bbox.

If any test fails, the algorithm re-rolls placement with the next seeded RNG offset (up to N=8 retries) before giving up and asking for human review.

### Random-but-optimal route between slots (multi-event flight) — placeholder

Designed after the per-slot algorithm is locked. Sketch:

- TSP-style ordering minimising total angular travel; constraints: promo slot is second; never two promos consecutive.
- Between adjacent slots: slide-with-mild-backout (no wide establishing).
- Between distant slots: faster fly-by (`ease-in-fast-ease-out`).
